About Alarmcast

In one line

Alarmcast is an Android alarm clock that wakes you with a podcast instead of a ringtone.

In a paragraph

Alarmcast is an alarm clock that wakes you with a podcast instead of a ringtone. Point an alarm at a show and it wakes you with that show's newest episode, or carries on where you left off. Press Listen on and the alarm stops while the episode keeps playing, so the conversation goes on as you get up. It's built for reliability: the alarm still rings after a phone restart, with no Wi-Fi, or when the phone has closed the app in the background. Once you're up, it's a full podcast player, with nearly 4,000 hand-picked shows to start from.

Facts

Works on
Android 7.0 or newer. There's no iPhone version.
Price
Free, with two alarms and ads in the app, never on the alarm screen. A one-time purchase removes the ads and the limit. Premium is a subscription with a 21-day free trial, or a one-time purchase.
Reliability
Four unique layers of reliability systems, and more than 300 safety checks. A free alarm is exactly as reliable as a paid one.

October 2026. See the listing
