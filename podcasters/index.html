How listeners use your code

They type it into the app and get a year of Premium, which drops the ads and lets the alarm pick up where they left off. Nobody is asked for a card.

Before you say yes

The mention needs to be marked as an ad, the way your show usually does it. Your listener code is an extra: share it on the show, in your show notes or on your site, or not at all. If you say it on air, say it's for Android and gives a free year, and point people to alarmcast.app/gift. Everything else is up to you, and if your episodes are short or your slots are tight, tell us and we'll work something out. Send us the episode link when it's out and your show goes into the next app update, where the order in Featured reshuffles every day.

We never ask anyone for a review or a rating. Saying yes lets us show your show's name and cover art in Featured while it's there, and we'll take it out whenever you ask. Children's shows can't take part.

A free year for you and your co-hosts

You get it whether or not you do the mention. Tell us how many of you make the show.
