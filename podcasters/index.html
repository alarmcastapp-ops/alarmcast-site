Your feed in Alarmcast

We built Alarmcast to support podcasters. The app works with Podcasting 2.0, the open standard from Podcast Index, which exists to preserve, protect and extend open, independent podcasting. Here's what Alarmcast sends to your host and reads from your feed.

Alarmcast is listed in the Podcast Index apps directory.

User-Agent
Alarmcast/<version> (Android) on feed checks, downloads and in-app plays. Podcast hosts name the apps in your stats from a shared list kept by the Open Podcast Analytics Working Group (OPAWG), and Alarmcast is on it.
podcast:trailer
If your trailer is one of the episodes in your feed, listeners who haven't subscribed get a Play trailer button on your show's Discover page. Apple's trailer episode type works too.
podcast:funding
Up to three support links appear on your show's About page.
podcast:transcript
An episode's notes open its transcript when you publish one as text, HTML, SRT, VTT or JSON.
podcast:podroll
The shows you recommend appear on your About page, when Podcast Index knows them. Listeners tap one to open it.
podcast:updateFrequency
Your About page shows the release schedule you publish.
podcast:block
If your feed asks not to be listed, we take your show out of Discover's topic lists and Featured at our next app update. Listeners can still find it by search.

How listeners use your code

They type it into the app and get a year of Premium, which drops the ads and lets the alarm pick up where they left off. Nobody is asked for a card.

Gift code terms

  1. 1In Settings, at the bottom, tap Enter a code.

  2. 2Type the code and tap Redeem.

Before you say yes

The mention needs to be marked as an ad, the way your show usually does it. Your listener code is an extra: share it on the show, in your show notes or on your site, or not at all. If you say it on air, say it's for Android and gives a free year, and point people to alarmcast.app. Everything else is up to you, and if your episodes are short or your slots are tight, tell us and we'll work something out. Send us the episode link when it's out and your show goes into the next app update, where the order in Featured reshuffles every day.

To see what you could say, the ad mention page has two ready-made versions, about 15 and 30 seconds long, with screenshots of the app.

We never ask anyone for a review or a rating. Saying yes lets us show your show's name and cover art in Featured while it's there, and we'll take it out whenever you ask. Children's shows can't take part.
